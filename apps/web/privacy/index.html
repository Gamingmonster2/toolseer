Privacy

The short version: we cannot read your files, because they never reach us.

What happens when you use a tool

Every tool on this site runs inside your browser. When you open a file, your browser reads it from your disk and keeps it in your computer's memory. The conversion code — the same JavaScript and WebAssembly that the page itself is made of — transforms the file locally and hands the result back to you as a download. At no point is the file, or any part of it, transmitted to a server.

This is not a policy choice; it is how the software is built. There is no upload step in the code, so there is nothing for a server to receive.

What is stored on your device

  • Theme (light or dark) and language preference, in your browser's localStorage.
  • A one-time flag recording that you have seen the disclaimer.
  • The page's static files (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WebAssembly) in the browser cache, exactly like any website.

OCR language data, when you choose to use OCR, is downloaded once and cached by your browser.

What we do not have

  • No accounts, so no account data.
  • No cookies set by us.
  • No analytics or advertising trackers on the tool pages.
  • No file storage. Deleted, cleared, or closed means gone from memory.

Hosting

The static files are served by Cloudflare Pages. As with any website, your browser's requests to load the page itself are visible to the hosting provider; the contents of your files are not part of those requests.
